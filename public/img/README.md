# Optional photos

Drop free-licensed photos here and the site picks them up automatically:

- `hero.jpg` — fills the right half of the hero, like the photo on a magazine cover. It's toned down to match the palette. A portrait, bookshelves or a record player all work.
- `desk.jpg` — sits faintly behind the activity log.

Use photos you took yourself or ones with a free license (Unsplash, Pexels).
Avoid real album covers, book covers and movie posters; they are copyrighted.
Keep each under ~400 KB (resize to about 1600px wide).
